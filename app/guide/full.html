Step 1 · once

Install it on your phone

It's a web app, so there's nothing to download from the App Store. It goes on your home screen like any other app.

  1. 1Open the link in Safari. On iPhone it has to be Safari; Chrome can't put web apps on the home screen. The link is the one I sent you.
  2. 2Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. The Share button is the square with the arrow at the bottom of Safari.
  3. 3From now on, open it from the icon, not from Safari. It runs full-screen and it works with no signal, so plant rooms and cellars are fine.
Step 2 · once

Three taps to set it up

The first time you open it, the Dashboard shows a Set up card. It ticks each one off and disappears when they're done.

  1. 1Your starting CTAP balance. The hours you're already up or down. This is the one that matters: without it every figure starts from zero, which is almost certainly not where you are. If you're genuinely level, enter 0 so it knows you've answered.
  2. 2This week's shifts. Tap Standard week if you're a normal Mon–Fri 08:00–16:30. Anything else, see the Schedule below.
  3. 3How the app works. Two minutes, and it covers where your data lives (short version: on your phone and nowhere else).
Schedule tab · each week

Set the week you're working

This is what sets your target. Get it right and everything else falls out of it.

  1. 1Pick the week with the arrows.
  2. 2Standard week sets Mon–Fri 08:00–16:30 in one tap.
  3. 3A day's times. Tap them to open the shift picker (next section), set start and finish, then Confirm.
  4. 4Leave. Annual leave or sickness. It takes that day's hours off your week, so you're not marked down for being off.
  5. 5Day note. Traffic, no access, training. It shows next to that day in the Forecast, so the context sits beside the numbers.
  6. 6Rest day. A day with no times is a normal day off in your rota. Unlike Leave it doesn't reduce your hours; you just aren't expected to earn that day.
  7. 7Default lunch. Taken off each day's target.
Schedule tab · tap a day's times

Set one day, copy it to the rest

Weeks don't have to be Monday to Friday. Monday to Thursday and Saturday is fine.

  1. 1Start and finish. Scroll or tap the hours and minutes. Nothing is saved yet.
  2. 2The shift and its hours after lunch, so you can check it before you save.
  3. 3Also apply to. Tap any other days to give them the same times. Days on leave can't be picked.
  4. 4Rest of week picks the weekdays after this one. Whole week picks every day.
  5. 5Confirm saves it and tells you how many days it's setting. Close with ✕ instead and nothing changes.
  6. 6Clear this day's times for a day you're not working. It becomes a rest day.
Log Job tab · each job

Log a job in one tap

The app opens here. Built to be used one-handed between calls.

  1. 1Search any job by name — across every category, wherever you are.
  2. 2Your week, Monday to Sunday, with the hours credited on each day. Tap a day to log against it if you forgot one; the arrows step back to a week you've finished.
  3. 3What's on that day. Everything you've logged to it, as you log it — so you can see the tap landed, and what it landed as. The ✕ takes one back off.
  4. 4Say what you've done. The blue bar. More on this next.
  5. 5Most used. Your six most-logged jobs. One tap logs it at its credit value.
Log Job tab · below Most used

Everything else, by category

The other 45 jobs live behind four tiles, so you're not scrolling one list of 51 to find the one you want.

  1. 1Browse all. Four categories, each saying how many jobs are in it.
  2. 2Gas and Hive. Services, repairs and certificates; smart controls and leads.
  3. 3SGO and Absence. Sales credits and fits; leave, NPT and mentor days.
Log Job tab · a category

Tap a tile, get the list

The category takes the whole screen. Tap a job and it's logged, and you stay where you are.

  1. 1Back to the tiles.
  2. 2The day this is going to. It follows you in, so a backdated job can't land on today by mistake. Tap it to pick a different day.
  3. 3Search from here too — it still looks across all four categories.
  4. 4Every job with its credit value. The ones marked Variable ask for a time first: wait work, NPT, trace and repair.
Log Job tab · the blue bar

Or just say it

Tap the bar and talk. Count first, then the job: “six breakdowns and two services”.

  1. 1Say the day, or the whole week. “Monday six breakdowns, Tuesday three services and an inhibitor, Wednesday a long duration.” Each day lands on the day you said. Take your time between jobs; it waits while you think.
  2. 2You get a draft, not a write. It reads back what it heard as a list. Change a count, swap a job, or remove one. Anything it couldn't place is shown to you, not quietly dropped. Nothing is logged until you tap Log.
  3. 3Name the appliance. “Four boiler repairs” is exact; “four repairs” is a guess, and the draft flags it in amber so you can check. Same with Hive: say “hive mini”, “hive wired” or “hive trvs”.
  4. 4Didn't catch you? Tap the mic to start again, or Type it instead. Your keyboard's own microphone key works in the box too.

Speech-to-text gets trade words wrong now and then. It knows the usual ones (“bank accounts” is breakdowns, “too high installs” is two Hive installs). If it writes something daft for a job you say a lot, tell me the exact words and I'll add it.

Dashboard tab · whenever

See where you stand today

Check it between jobs. Everything on it comes from what you've logged.

  1. 1Week switcher. Go back to look at or fix an earlier week.
  2. 2Job credits. Earned today, still needed to hit today's target, and the week gap. The bar fills towards today's target, and the line under it says what would close it. “5 more Gas Repair closes today's gap.”
  3. 3Daily check-in. One private question a day, under a minute. Nothing to do with CTAP; more on it below.
  4. 4Coach. Reads your own figures back: streaks, balance, what's left this week, and what the extras on a visit are worth.
  5. 5CTAP balance. Your running credit (green) or deficit (red) in hours, starting from the balance you set. Tap it for the Cash-Out.
  6. 6Week. Hours banked against target, a bar for each day. The % goes green when you're on pace for the day you're on, not just at the end of the week. Switch between Actual and Projected. Tap it for the Weekly Forecast.
  7. 7Today's jobs. Everything logged today with its credit. Tap ✕ to remove one, or + Add a job at the bottom.
Dashboard · tap the CTAP tile

What your balance is worth in your pocket

Slides up from the bottom. Swipe down or tap ✕ to close.

  1. 1Payable balance. Your CTAP credit in hours. In deficit it shows £0, because there's nothing to cash out yet.
  2. 2Your multiplier. 0×, 0.8×, 1.4× or 2×. Pick yours.
  3. 3Tax band. Gross, Basic (20% tax + 8% NI) or Higher (40% tax + 2% NI).
  4. 4Gross and take-home, with the calculation shown underneath so you can check it. 17.23h × £19.39 × 1.4 − 28% = £336.83.

Personal estimate only. Check it against your payslip; the app doesn't see your pay.

Dashboard · tap the Week tile

The week, day by day

Where the week's heading at your current pace, and what you need each day from here.

  1. 1Day strip. Monday to Sunday with each day's hours. Rest days show as Rest. Opens on today; tap any day.
  2. 2That day's jobs with the credit for each. Tap ✕ to remove one. Any day note from the Schedule shows here too.
  3. 3Edit Times. Fix the time on a job you logged late.
  4. 4Credits earned against the week's target, and where the week landed or is heading.
  5. 5Projected, daily average, days worked, days left. Days left only counts days you're working, so leave and rest days don't thin out the target.
Coach Mode · on the Dashboard

Coach works out the way back

Say you're 8.40h in deficit on a Wednesday morning. This is what Coach does with that.

  1. 1Your run rate. It spreads the deficit over the next 20 working days, so it's a small daily top-up rather than one impossible week. 8.40h ÷ 20 days = +0.42h a day above target. It also tells you whether last week shrank the deficit or added to it.
  2. 2Today, in jobs. Job Credits turns what's left of today's target into jobs, using the credit of the last job you logged. 2.05h still needed ÷ 0.93h per Gas Repair = 3 more.
  3. 3The rest of the week. The Forecast takes your daily average so far, shows where the week finishes at that pace, and gives you the daily rate to still make target. 14.94h to go ÷ 2 days left = 7.47h a day.
  4. 4What lifts the balance most. After a service or repair it lists the best advice you could give on that visit, a Hive, an inhibitor, a CO alarm, a boiler lead, and what each is worth against today's gap. Never dispatched work: it won't suggest a service or a repair you weren't given.

Coach only uses your own logged figures. Turn it off in Settings if you'd rather just have the numbers.

Daily check-in · on the Dashboard

An honest diary only you can read

One question a day, under a minute. It has nothing to do with your CTAP figures.

  1. 1Built on GROW. Goal, Reality, Options, Will. Monday asks what you want to work on this week; the rest of the week asks how it's going. Five days read as one conversation.
  2. 2It's for you. Where the gaps in the day were, what you're struggling with, what you'd do differently. Three answers: not really, so-so, yes. Skip it any day you like.
  3. 3Nobody else sees it. No team view, no export, no comparing engineers. That's how it's built, not a setting. Off in Settings if it isn't for you.
History tab

Every week you've logged

The pattern over time, and the balance that carries from week to week.

  1. 1Weekly trend. The last six weeks. Green means bonus hit. “4 / 6 weeks bonus.”
  2. 2Each week: hours against target, with a dot for the result: green is bonus, amber is close, red is below. Tap a week to open it.
  3. 3In CTAP. Had a week that wasn't a fair test? Long sickness, a phased return. Untick it and it stays out of your balance without being deleted.
  4. 4Travel. Note that week's travel hours. For now it's a record only; the 80% target already allows for travel.
Settings tab · once

Set once, then leave it alone

  1. 1Your name. Only used for the greeting.
  2. 2Appearance. Dark or light, and the switches for Coach and the daily check-in.
  3. 3Weekly hours and Target %. Your rostered hours and the share of them you're aiming to credit. 80% is the default; tap the i for why.
  4. 4Starting CTAP balance. The same one from set-up. The +/− button switches it between credit and deficit.
  5. 5How to use this app. A short version of this guide, built in.
  6. 6On-device. Your data stays on this phone. Erase all data is further down: tap Erase, type ERASE, confirm.
Settings · Appearance

Dark mode, if you'd rather

Everything in this guide is shown in light mode. The app works exactly the same in dark; only the colours change.

  1. 1Settings → Appearance → Theme. Pick Dark or Light. It's saved on your phone, so it stays how you left it.
  2. 2Same screens, same taps. Every step above applies either way, so nothing here needs re-reading if you switch.
Before you start

Your figures never leave your phone

No account, no server, nothing uploaded. Not to me, not to your manager, not to anyone.

  1. 1It's completely secure because there's nothing to secure. Once it's installed the app makes no network requests at all. Nobody can see your CTAP figures but you.
  2. 2That also means there's no backup. Delete the app, erase the data or lose the phone and it's gone. Nobody can get it back, because nobody else ever had it.
  3. 3It's a personal tool. The figures are estimates from what you log. Check anything that matters against your payslip and the company systems.

Don't delete the app to “reinstall” it. Deleting it deletes your data. If something looks wrong, message me first.
